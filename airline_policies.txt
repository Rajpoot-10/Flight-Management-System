FLIGHT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
AIRLINE PASSENGER POLICIES
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: September 2026

==================================================
1. GENERAL POLICY
==================================================

These policies apply to bookings created through the Flight Management System.

Passengers are responsible for providing accurate personal and booking information.

All bookings, cancellations, refunds, seat assignments, waitlist offers, and notifications are subject to flight availability and the fare rules associated with the booking.

The system currently supports three travel classes:

- First Class
- Business Class
- Economy Class

The system supports two fare types:

- Basic Fare
- Flexible Fare


==================================================
2. BASIC FARE POLICY
==================================================

Basic Fare is the restricted fare option available for First Class, Business Class, and Economy Class.

Basic Fare rules:

- Basic Fare bookings are non-refundable.
- Voluntary booking changes are not allowed.
- Passenger seat choice is not included.
- Cancellation refund percentage is 0%.
- Cancelling a Basic Fare booking does not generate a passenger refund.
- Any seat released after cancellation may become available to another passenger or an eligible waitlisted passenger.

These restrictions apply regardless of whether the passenger booked First Class, Business Class, or Economy Class using the Basic Fare.


==================================================
3. FLEXIBLE FARE POLICY
==================================================

Flexible Fare provides more flexibility than Basic Fare.

Flexible Fare rules:

- Flexible Fare bookings are refundable.
- Booking changes are allowed.
- Passenger seat choice is allowed.
- The configured cancellation refund percentage is 100%.
- When an eligible Flexible Fare booking is cancelled, the system calculates the refund according to the applicable fare rule.
- The released seat may become available to another passenger or an eligible waitlisted passenger.

These rules apply to First Class, Business Class, and Economy Class Flexible Fare bookings.


==================================================
4. BOOKING POLICY
==================================================

A booking can only be confirmed when the required seat hold is valid and active.

A temporary seat hold does not represent a confirmed booking.

A booking is confirmed only after the booking confirmation process completes successfully.

The system uses idempotency protection to reduce accidental duplicate booking creation.

A booking is associated with:

- a flight
- a passenger
- a travel class
- a fare type
- a payment status
- an assigned seat

The requested travel class must have valid inventory.


==================================================
5. TEMPORARY SEAT HOLD POLICY
==================================================

Passengers may temporarily hold an available physical seat before confirming a booking.

A seat hold is valid for approximately 10 minutes.

While the hold is active:

- the physical seat is marked as held
- another passenger should not be assigned the same physical seat

If the booking is successfully confirmed before expiry:

- the seat becomes booked
- the seat hold becomes completed

If the hold expires before booking confirmation:

- the hold is marked as expired
- the physical seat is released
- the seat may become available to another passenger

Expired seat holds are automatically processed by the background automation system.


==================================================
6. CANCELLATION POLICY
==================================================

Passengers may request cancellation of an eligible booking.

The financial result of cancellation depends on the fare type associated with the booking.

Basic Fare:

- refund percentage: 0%
- the booking may be cancelled
- no passenger refund is generated under the standard voluntary cancellation rule

Flexible Fare:

- refund percentage: 100%
- eligible cancellations generate a refund based on the configured fare rule

After successful cancellation:

- the booking is marked as cancelled
- the passenger booking status is updated
- the associated seat is released
- seat inventory is restored
- an eligible refund record is generated when the refund amount is greater than zero


==================================================
7. REFUND POLICY
==================================================

Refund eligibility is determined by the fare rule associated with the booking.

A refund record may contain:

- booking information
- passenger information
- refund type
- refund amount
- refund status
- refund reason
- request time
- processing time

Basic Fare voluntary cancellation:

Refund Percentage: 0%

Flexible Fare eligible cancellation:

Refund Percentage: 100%

Refund processing must be recorded in the Flight Management System database.


==================================================
8. WAITLIST POLICY
==================================================

Passengers may join a flight waitlist when their requested travel class has no available inventory.

A waitlist request includes:

- flight
- passenger
- travel class
- fare type
- priority information

Passengers waiting for the same flight and class are considered according to the system's configured priority ordering.

When inventory becomes available, the system may offer the seat opportunity to the highest-priority eligible waiting passenger.

The system uses database row locking during automated promotion to reduce the risk of the same waitlist opportunity being processed concurrently.


==================================================
9. WAITLIST OFFER AND CLAIM POLICY
==================================================

When a passenger is promoted from the waitlist:

- the waitlist entry becomes offered
- an offer expiration time is created
- the passenger receives approximately 10 minutes to claim the offer

An offer does not itself constitute a confirmed booking.

When an eligible passenger successfully claims an active offer:

- an available physical seat is selected
- a temporary seat hold is created
- the physical seat becomes held
- the waitlist entry becomes claimed

The passenger must still complete the normal booking confirmation process.

Expired or invalid waitlist offers cannot be claimed.


==================================================
10. FLIGHT STATUS POLICY
==================================================

Flights may use the following operational statuses:

- scheduled
- delayed
- cancelled
- completed

Passenger-facing automated reminders are only generated for eligible scheduled flights.

Cancelled or otherwise ineligible flights must not generate normal check-in reminders.


==================================================
11. CHECK-IN REMINDER POLICY
==================================================

The system may automatically send check-in reminders for upcoming flights.

A reminder is generated when:

- the booking is confirmed
- payment status is paid
- the flight is scheduled
- the flight falls within the configured upcoming departure window

The current automated reminder window is approximately 24 hours before departure.

The system records sent reminders in the notification database.

A reminder already recorded for the same booking and notification type should not be sent repeatedly.

Cancelled flights are excluded from normal check-in reminders.


==================================================
12. PRICE DROP ALERT POLICY
==================================================

Passengers may create a price alert for a specific:

- flight
- travel class
- target price

Price alerts are available for scheduled flights.

The system periodically compares the current base fare with the passenger's target price.

A price-drop alert becomes eligible when:

Current Fare <= Passenger Target Price

When the condition is satisfied:

- the passenger may receive a Gmail notification
- the notified fare is recorded as the last notified price

The system uses the last notified price to prevent repeated notifications for the same fare.

If the fare changes and later satisfies the alert conditions at a different price, another notification may be generated.


==================================================
13. SEAT INVENTORY POLICY
==================================================

Each flight maintains separate inventory for:

- First Class
- Business Class
- Economy Class

Inventory records track:

- total seats
- available seats
- base fare

Available inventory must not become negative.

A successfully confirmed booking reduces the applicable available class inventory.

A successful cancellation restores the applicable class inventory.

Physical seat status and class-level inventory are maintained as related but distinct operational records.


==================================================
14. PHYSICAL SEAT POLICY
==================================================

Each physical seat belongs to a specific flight and travel class.

Seat statuses may represent operational states such as:

- available
- held
- booked

A seat marked as booked cannot be assigned to another active booking.

A held seat is temporarily reserved while its corresponding seat hold remains valid.

Expired holds release their associated physical seats.


==================================================
15. PRICE AND FARE POLICY
==================================================

Each flight class may have a configured base fare.

The fare displayed or used by the system may depend on the applicable flight, travel class, and fare configuration.

Price alerts compare their target price against the current base fare stored for the requested flight class.

All monetary values used by this demonstration system are system-configured values and do not represent the pricing policies of any real airline.


==================================================
16. NOTIFICATION POLICY
==================================================

The system may generate operational passenger notifications including:

- check-in reminders
- price-drop alerts

Notification activity may be logged in Supabase.

Notification records may include:

- passenger
- booking
- notification type
- recipient email
- subject
- message
- notification status
- scheduled time
- sent time

De-duplication controls should be used where applicable to reduce repeated notifications.


==================================================
17. BAGGAGE POLICY
==================================================

No production baggage allowance rules have currently been configured for this demonstration Flight Management System.

If a passenger asks about exact baggage weight, baggage dimensions, excess baggage fees, or class-specific baggage allowances, the system must not invent an answer.

Such questions should be identified as requiring an airline-approved baggage policy or human review.


==================================================
18. HUMAN REVIEW POLICY
==================================================

AI-generated policy answers must be based on retrieved policy information.

If the available policy information does not clearly answer a passenger's question, the system should not invent a rule.

Unclear, unsupported, exceptional, or conflicting cases should be escalated for human review.

Where human approval is required, an AI-generated response must not be sent to the passenger until the designated reviewer approves it.


==================================================
19. RAG RESPONSE POLICY
==================================================

The AI policy assistant must use retrieved policy context when answering passenger questions.

The assistant should:

- answer only from relevant retrieved policy information
- avoid inventing airline policies
- distinguish Basic Fare from Flexible Fare
- consider the passenger's actual booking fare type when booking information is available
- provide clear explanations of cancellation and refund eligibility
- escalate questions when the retrieved information is insufficient
- require human approval before sending responses where the workflow requires approval

Examples:

Question:
"Can I get a refund for my Basic Fare booking?"

Expected policy answer:
Under the configured Basic Fare policy, voluntary cancellation has a 0% refund percentage, so the standard Basic Fare cancellation does not generate a refund.


Question:
"I booked Flexible Fare. Can I cancel it?"

Expected policy answer:
Yes. Under the configured Flexible Fare policy, the booking is refundable and the configured cancellation refund percentage is 100%, subject to the booking being eligible for cancellation.


Question:
"How much baggage can I carry?"

Expected policy answer:
The current policy knowledge base does not define a production baggage allowance. The request should be referred for human review rather than providing an unsupported baggage limit.


==================================================
20. POLICY DISCLAIMER
==================================================

These policies are demonstration policies created specifically for the Flight Management System capstone project.

They are not the official policies of any real airline.

Operational rules stored in the system database remain authoritative for live booking transactions.

Where retrieved policy text conflicts with validated transactional data or an approved operational rule, the case should be escalated for review.
